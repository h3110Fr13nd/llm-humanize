## 🎯 Research Objective
Integrate the stabilized equilibrium state of the cyclic concept graph into a designated surface decoder node that generates humanized, grammatically fluent text.

## 🧪 Experimental Questions:
1. **Interface Mechanics**: How should the cyclic graph's multi-node attractor states be pooled and projected into the decoder node's residual stream?
2. **Decoupled Truth Verification**: Does isolating conceptual reasoning inside the cyclic graph prevent the surface decoder node from hallucinating non-existent entities?
3. **Fluency vs. Settling Iterations**: Measure how surface rewrite fluency improves as a function of the internal cyclic settling budget $\mathcal{T}_{\text{settle}}$.
